Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

SoloBiz OS – Business Manager is designed to help small businesses manage invoices, quotations, customers, expenses and reports.

Information We Store

The app may store business details, customer names, phone numbers, invoice details, quotations and expense records entered by users.

Data Storage

Currently, your business records are stored locally in your browser on your device. SoloBiz OS does not currently provide cloud synchronization. Data may be lost if browser storage is cleared or the device is reset. Users are encouraged to download backups regularly.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide business management features such as invoicing, quotations, expense tracking, sales reports and PDF generation.

Sharing of Information

SoloBiz OS does not sell personal information. Users may choose to share invoices or business information through WhatsApp or other apps using the sharing features.

Security

Users should protect their devices and avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information. Since data is stored locally in the browser, device and browser security are important.

Children's Privacy

SoloBiz OS is intended for business owners and is not designed for children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change.
